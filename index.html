01 — About

More than a dance class.
A space to move and belong.

Established in 2016 in Haralur (HSR Extension), Clouds Dance Studio was founded on a simple belief: dance is for everyone. Whether you’re stepping onto a studio floor for the very first time, looking to sweat off weekday stress, or refining your performance craft, our doors are open.

We blend disciplined movement training with vibrant Indian urban energy. From beginner-friendly step-by-step breakdowns to camera-ready choreography routines, we ensure every dancer leaves feeling energized, confident, and inspired.

05 — Spaces

The studio experience.

Come for the class.
Stay for the community.

A physical sanctuary on Haralur Main Road designed for focused movement, sound clarity, and human connection.

1,500+ Sq. Ft. Sprung Floor

Shock-absorbent wooden spring flooring engineered to protect joints during jumps and routines.

Wall-to-Wall Acoustic Mirrors

Distortion-free mirrors spanning the length of the hall for posture alignment and line correction.

Immersive Sound & Lighting

High-definition audio and programmable ambient lighting that brings stage atmosphere to practice.

AC Lounge & Changing Rooms

Full air-conditioning, clean private changing lounges, and fresh drinking water facilities.
